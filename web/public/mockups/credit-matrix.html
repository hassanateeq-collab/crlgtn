Credit matrix — who books on credit, where

Corlington stays merchant of record: corporates always owe Corlington, hotels are always settled monthly. This matrix governs where Corlington extends its credit, because the default-split clause in each hotel agreement shares that risk with the property. Where credit doesn't apply, booking still works — prepaid or from the standing deposit.

Hotel credit tiers (set by the signed agreement)

The tier is agreed at onboarding and recorded on the hotel's agreement.
HT1 · OPEN

Credit for every approved corporate (A, B and C).

HT2 · STANDARD

Credit for corporate tiers A and B. Tier C prepays.

HT3 · SELECTIVE

Credit for tier A only. B and C prepay.

HT4 · PREPAID

No shared credit risk — all corporates prepay or draw deposit.

The cash-flow rule (owner decision 2026-08-18)

Corporate money always lands before hotel money leaves. Hotels are settled within 30 days of month-end; corporate credit is capped below it — A ≤ d20 · B ≤ d15 · C = on-checkout / d7 — dated from checkout. Worst case (checkout on the 31st, tier A): client cash due day 20, hotel paid day 30 — a 10-day buffer on every booking. Corlington never finances the float. d30 corporate terms are abolished.

Effective policy — hotels × corporates

Defaults come from hotel tier × corporate tier. Click any cell to override the pair: once = force credit ✓, twice = force prepaid ✕, three times = back to tier default.
✓ credit — invoice on the corporate's terms ⊘ prepaid — bank transfer or deposit drawdown before confirmation OVERRIDE — pair-level exception beats the tier
