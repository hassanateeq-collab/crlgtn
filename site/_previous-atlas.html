How it works

Three steps, one invoice.

No back-and-forth on email, no chasing a rate that changed since last quarter, no reconciling a dozen separate hotel bills at month end.

01

File the request

Dates, corridor, rooms and the package you need — room only, with breakfast, or half board. It takes about a minute.

02

Three hotels respond

We put the request to three verified properties at once. They answer inside a fixed window — three hours as standard, one hour when you are inside 48 hours of check-in.

03

Book and settle monthly

Pick the offer you want. The voucher issues immediately with the policies attached, and the stay joins one consolidated invoice on your agreed credit terms.

For companies

Your travel desk, without the desk.

  • Vetted supply only. Every property is visited, photographed to a fixed shot list and its amenities confirmed on site — not copied from a brochure.
  • Rates agreed in advance. You see the contracted rate for the room, not a surge price discovered at checkout.
  • Approval built in. Turn on countersign and bookings above your threshold go to your nominated approver before they are final.
  • One monthly invoice. Consolidated, itemised, on your credit terms, settled by bank transfer.
  • Airport transfers at fixed contracted prices by zone, with meet-and-greet and a waiting allowance built into the rule.

For hotels & operators

Corporate demand, no dashboard to learn.

  • Answer on WhatsApp. A request arrives as a message with a link. One tap to accept, counter or decline. No account, no password, no portal.
  • You set the ceiling. Your rate card is countersigned before you go live and nothing is ever sold below it.
  • Rate parity and non-circumvention are written into the agreement, and they bind both directions.
  • No listing fee. Commission is a share of gross on bookings that actually complete.
  • Paid on agreed terms against a reconciled settlement run — not whenever a corporate client gets round to it.
