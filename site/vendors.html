Commercial terms

Written down before you go live.

Everything below is in the agreement you countersign at the onboarding visit. None of it changes without you signing again.

01

No listing fee

Nothing is charged to be on the platform. Commission is a share of gross, taken only on bookings that actually complete.

02

You set the ceiling

Your rate card sets the maximum per category. Nothing is ever sold below it, and negotiated corporate rates sit inside your own ceiling.

03

Parity and non-circumvention

Both are written into the agreement, and both bind in each direction — us to you as much as you to us.

04

Paid against a reconciled run

Settlement is reconciled to the rupee and gated on receipts before it is released, on the terms in your agreement.

05

Your identity stays yours

Corporate clients book against verified categories and areas. What we publish about you is what the agreement says we may.

06

No exclusivity

Listing with Corlington does not stop you selling through any other channel you already use.
